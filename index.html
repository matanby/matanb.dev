About

I'm a generative-AI researcher and engineer at LTX, Lightricks' video model team. I work on post-training for LTX-2 — mostly controllability: IC-LoRAs, ControlNet-style adapters, LoRA training for things like video detailing and spatiotemporal super-resolution, and performance optimization of the training stack. I designed and built the LTX Trainer from scratch — the training system we and the community use to fine-tune LTX models. Earlier at Lightricks I worked across the stack — LoRA training infrastructure for SD and Flux at large scale, and the backend of a text-to-image system serving millions of users.

I've been building software for a long time, across quite a few domains. Before LTX I worked on deep-learning and computer-vision research at D-ID, where I built the face-animation models behind Live Portraits and AI Avatars, and worked on adversarial face de-identification and privacy-preserving vision. I also built FaceFX, a GAN-based face-effects app acquired by Lightricks at the end of 2022 — which is how I ended up here. Earlier I spent years as a software engineer well outside of AI.

I care about simple, well-designed software, and I'll spend real time getting the systems I build right. I've been writing code since I was ten, and outside work I run a home lab I enjoy over-engineering.
